# SK-02 — Premium Motion Design (`v1.0.0`)

- **Skill ID**: `SK-02`
- **Intent**: Cohesive, high-craft art direction and elevated perceived quality for product storytelling, brand education, and design-forward explainers across any niche.
- **Pacing Principles**:
  - Measured, intentional choreography with generous breathing room around hero visual settles.
  - Prioritize spatial continuity and unified grid alignment over rapid visual churn.
- **Hook Strategy Pool**: `editorial_hero_reveal`, `precision_typographic_lockup`, `tactile_artifact_spotlight`.
- **Footage Grammar**: Curated high-resolution verified product/source imagery and clean masked B-roll frames matching derived palette; strictly forbids fabricated UI screens or fake app mockups.
- **Explanation Strategy Pool**: Coherent editorial data cards, refined proportional reservoirs, crisp labeled comparison matrices, schematic exploded relationships clearly marked `CONCEPT_ILLUSTRATION`.
- **Motion & Art Direction**: Derived source/brand palette, 1–2 licensed type families, crisp hairlines, directional mask wipes, shape morphs, subtle shadow depth, restrained easing (`power3.out` / `power2.inOut`) without cheap neon spam or fake 3D claims.
- **Audio Strategy**: Refined tactile textures (soft airy whooshes, subtle UI clicks, warm low impacts, restrained completion chime, optional subtle licensed underscore) with wide headroom (`<= -1.0 dBTP`).
- **Failure Modes**: Ornamental complexity hurting readability, repetitive glass-card spam, sacrificing factual explanation for aesthetics.
- **Fallback Policy**: When product screenshots/interior pages are unavailable, use verified official cover + clean typographic hierarchy + honest `EVIDENCE_GAPS.md`.
