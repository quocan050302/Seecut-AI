# SK-01 — Viral Dynamic Editing (`v1.0.0`)

- **Skill ID**: `SK-01`
- **Intent**: High-energy editorial clarity, pattern interruption, and truthful curiosity-to-payoff momentum across any topic.
- **Pacing Principles**:
  - Adapt visual-change cadence to semantic density (never a mechanical every-N-second cut timer).
  - Open within 0–3s with a truthful contradiction, stakes frame, surprising mechanism, or crisp problem statement complementing spoken audio.
  - Preserve natural voice rhythm under `LOCK_VOICE` or safe `PRESERVE_SCRIPT_TRIM` (zero mid-word cuts).
- **Hook Strategy Pool**: `contradiction_type_lockup`, `stakes_first_visual_proof`, `counterintuitive_mechanism_tease`.
- **Footage Grammar**: Timely supporting B-roll / real evidence inserts in permitted windows or overlays; never obscures face, hands, or active source action; every external clip requires verified rights and clear relevance.
- **Explanation Strategy Pool**: Progressive proportional bars, animated rolling values (exact `Decimal` arithmetic), crisp contrast comparisons, punchy cause-to-effect arrows.
- **Motion & Art Direction**: Bold typographic contrast, directional clip wipes, responsive kinetic phrase highlights, clean micro-punches; zero seizure-like flashes, zero random zooms, zero unreadable word-by-word karaoke spam.
- **Audio Strategy**: Rhythmic motivated accents (hook impact, contradiction zip, allocation/step ticks, transition sweeps, payoff chime) strictly ducked beneath voice; zero SFX-per-word clutter.
- **Failure Modes**: Over-cutting, effect spam competing with speech, spoiling payoff on frame 0, fake FOMO/scarcity.
- **Fallback Policy**: When external B-roll or licensed SFX is unavailable, fall back cleanly to A-roll + original semantic motion graphics (`FOOTAGE_PARTIAL` / `BLOCKED_SFX_ASSETS`), keeping voice crystal clear.
