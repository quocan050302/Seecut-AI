# SK-04 — Intelligent Visual Explainer (`v1.0.0`)

- **Skill ID**: `SK-04`
- **Intent**: Maximize genuine audience comprehension of mechanisms, numeric relationships, causes, workflows, and decisions across any niche.
- **Pacing Principles**:
  - Progress deliberately from known premise -> intermediate mechanism -> verified conclusion; never rush numeric or causal reveals faster than mobile reading speed.
  - Hold resolved states long enough for viewers to verify the relationship themselves.
- **Hook Strategy Pool**: `paradox_equation_hook`, `mechanism_question_frame`, `misconception_vs_reality_split`.
- **Footage Grammar**: Real demonstrations, verified screenshots/captures, and physical process clips anchored directly to explanation nodes; labeled `CONCEPT_ILLUSTRATION` whenever schematic.
- **Explanation Strategy Pool**: Typed causal flow graphs, exact `Decimal`-checked proportional allocation bars, rolling tabular arithmetic counters, annotated step sequences, side-by-side comparison matrices, recap summaries.
- **Motion & Art Direction**: High-contrast legibility on `360px` mobile screens, color ALWAYS paired with explicit labels/patterns/icons (never color-only), one coherent evolving visual model instead of unrelated card stacks.
- **Audio Strategy**: Clean semantic step cues (discrete allocation ticks, subtle paper/mechanism foley, clear payoff resolution chime) keeping spoken explanations 100% intelligible.
- **Failure Modes**: Captions-only treatment of hard concepts, wrong arithmetic or misleading chart proportions, decorative diagrams that fail to clarify the mechanism.
- **Fallback Policy**: If live UI captures or external B-roll are missing, visualize exact verified mechanics via labeled `CONCEPT_ILLUSTRATION` diagrams without fabricating fake UI.
