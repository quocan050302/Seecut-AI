# SK-05 — Natural Retention Editing (`v1.0.0`)

- **Skill ID**: `SK-05`
- **Intent**: Preserve authentic human presence, voice cadence, and real-world physical action while sustaining viewer engagement through restrained, intentional craft across any niche.
- **Pacing Principles**:
  - Edit around natural breaths, eye contact, expressions, and physical action continuity (pouring, folding, sorting, cooking, walking); never create visual teleportation across cuts.
  - Keep overlays lightweight so the creator and physical workspace remain the unmistakable visual anchor.
- **Hook Strategy Pool**: `authentic_presence_plus_compact_tension`, `real_action_open_loop`, `conversational_curiosity_line`.
- **Footage Grammar**: Selective contextual insets and brief supporting visuals placed strictly in verified negative space; preserves real physical open loops without manufacturing fake outcomes.
- **Explanation Strategy Pool**: Compact evolving top/edge ledger, subtle tracked/negative-space annotations, clean concise comparison strips, truthful payoff highlight without dimming the speaker.
- **Motion & Art Direction**: Warm natural palette harmonized with source footage, understated typography, restrained micro-motion, box-free or compact pills strictly outside face/hands/props.
- **Audio Strategy**: Preserve natural room/prop foley as foundation; add only selective, tasteful micro-accents and gentle ducked ambience/underscore when licensed and helpful.
- **Failure Modes**: Zero visual clarity on complex concepts ("minimal" mistaken for unedited), aggressive sales badges clashing with creator authenticity, covering active hands or props.
- **Fallback Policy**: Works natively on pure A-roll + subtle original motion graphics + preserved source sound; marks optional missing external B-roll/SFX cleanly without blocking.
