# SK-03 — Cinematic Storytelling (`v1.0.0`)

- **Skill ID**: `SK-03`
- **Intent**: Narrative progression, atmosphere, causal sequence, emotional stakes, and earned payoff — scaling cleanly from compact 20s stories to multi-minute essays across any niche.
- **Pacing Principles**:
  - Respect emotional cadence, deliberate holds, and dramatic silence where earned.
  - Use J/L-style audio bridges and motivated scene transitions ONLY when source cut policy permits and A/V sync stays exact.
- **Hook Strategy Pool**: `in_media_res_question`, `atmospheric_stakes_anchor`, `visual_callback_setup`.
- **Footage Grammar**: Atmospheric contextual B-roll, source-backed maps/timelines, and visual callbacks clearly distinguishing verified primary evidence vs labeled illustrative reconstruction (zero fabricated archival footage).
- **Explanation Strategy Pool**: Chronological timelines with validated dates/boundaries, actor-cause-consequence chains, before/after grounded in real assets.
- **Motion & Art Direction**: Restrained warm archival/editorial palettes, editorial serif headlines, smooth zero-overshoot eases (`power2.inOut` / `power2.out`), minimal chrome, subtle light contrast.
- **Audio Strategy**: Natural ambience continuity, soft sound bridges, restrained tension risers/releases, intentional silence before key turns; voice timbre untouched.
- **Failure Modes**: Forcing 3-act film tropes onto a 15s utility clip, dramatizing unsupported facts, obscuring authentic facial expressions.
- **Fallback Policy**: Without archival/contextual B-roll, anchor atmosphere on A-roll framing, timeline diagrams, and clean soundscape while recording `FOOTAGE_PARTIAL`.
